Frequently Asked Policy Questions

How is my estimated premium computed?

Your premium is calculated dynamically using a transparent base rate adjusted by your vehicle type (multiplier), vehicle age risk factor, and selected optional add-ons. We enforce absolute structural pricing integrity.

What happens once my proposal is submitted?

The proposal enters our digital underwriting queue. A dedicated officer inspects credentials, verifies asset records, audits documents, adjusts modifiers if exceptions apply, and delivers a final verified quote immediately.

Can I transition from liability to comprehensive later?

Yes. You can edit pending proposals or submit new comprehensive covers for active registered vehicles through the Customer Dashboard at any time.
